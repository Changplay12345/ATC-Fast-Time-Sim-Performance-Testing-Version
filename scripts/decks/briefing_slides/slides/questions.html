Questions management may ask

What does it cost?

One Claude subscription. GitHub is free at our size. No server needed for building and delivery.

Is our work confidential?

Code passes through Anthropic’s service under business terms. Only test traffic, no real operational data. The signing key never leaves the engineer’s PC.

Are we dependent on these tools?

No more than on any supplier. Everything is ordinary files; another engineer can continue from the notes.

Can an AI be trusted with the code?

Its work is reviewed and tested like any engineer’s. Its mistakes were caught by the tests and the review before release.

Does it replace the engineer?

No. Less typing and routine checking; more deciding, reviewing and measuring.
